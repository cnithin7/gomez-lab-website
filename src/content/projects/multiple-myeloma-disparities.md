## Background

Multiple myeloma incidence and precursor conditions differ substantially across populations, including a higher burden among African American patients.

## What we are asking

We investigate genomic, transcriptomic, ancestry-related, and tumor microenvironment differences that may help explain disparities in multiple myeloma.
