## Background

Hodgkin lymphoma contains a rare malignant cell population that has historically made molecular characterization difficult.

## What we are asking

We use cutting-edge genomic technologies to resolve the pathobiology of Hodgkin lymphoma and related lymphoid malignancies, including somatic variation and shared founding precursors.
